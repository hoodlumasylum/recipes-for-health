# Recipes for Health

A small, herb-led recipe site from Hoodlum Asylum. Drinks, teas, kitchen dishes and fruit blends, written for flavour.

Live site:

**https://hoodlumasylum.github.io/recipes-for-health/**

## What’s on the site

- Featured recipe: Egyptian hibiscus drink (karkadeh), served hot or cold
- Herbal teas and simple herby kitchen dishes
- A **Blends** section of fruit and herb smoothies (mango-mint, strawberry-basil, pineapple-ginger, berry-hibiscus, banana-cinnamon)
- A **Traditional herbs** section: damiana tea and mugwort tea, described only as folk tradition, not as something that works
- A short note on every visit: these are recipes, not medical advice

The site does not make medical or health claims, and it does not include CBD or blue lotus. Mugwort tea carries a warning: do not drink it if you are pregnant, trying to conceive or breastfeeding, and avoid it if you are allergic to the daisy or ragweed family. The site also says to check with a doctor or pharmacist if you take medication.
