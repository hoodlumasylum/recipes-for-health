# Recipes for Health

A small, herb-led recipe site from Hoodlum Asylum. Drinks, teas, kitchen dishes and fruit blends, written for flavour.

Live site:

**https://hoodlumasylum.github.io/recipes-for-health/**

## What’s on the site

- Featured recipe: Egyptian hibiscus drink (karkadeh), served hot or cold
- Herbal teas and simple herby kitchen dishes
- A **Blends** section of fruit and herb smoothies (mango-mint, strawberry-basil, pineapple-ginger, berry-hibiscus, banana-cinnamon)
- A short note on every visit: these are recipes, not medical advice

The site does not make medical or health claims, and it does not include CBD.
